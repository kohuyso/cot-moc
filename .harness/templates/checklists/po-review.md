# Product Review Checklist: PO Deliverables

**Target Artifact**: `artifacts/product-review.md`
**Reviewer Role**: `sdlc-po-review`
**Marker Semantics**: Mark `[x]` only when the product alignment criterion is satisfied.

---

## 1. Business Value & Customer Alignment
- [ ] `CHK-PO-001`: Does this change solve a confirmed customer or business need aligned with roadmap priorities?
- [ ] `CHK-PO-002`: Are target personas and user journeys realistically represented?
- [ ] `CHK-PO-003`: Is the priority classification (P0/P1/P2) justified by business urgency or regulatory compliance?

## 2. MVP Scope & Trade-offs
- [ ] `CHK-PO-004`: Is the MVP slice strictly minimal, deferring nice-to-have features to future releases?
- [ ] `CHK-PO-005`: Are trade-offs between delivery timeline and functional depth explicitly acknowledged?
- [ ] `CHK-PO-006`: Are non-goals respected without backdoor scope creep?

## 3. Readiness & Go-To-Market
- [ ] `CHK-PO-007`: Are operational and customer-facing documentation impacts accounted for?
- [ ] `CHK-PO-008`: Are release telemetry and success metrics defined to measure post-launch value?
- [ ] `CHK-PO-009`: Is the definition of ready (DoR) fully satisfied for technical handoff?
